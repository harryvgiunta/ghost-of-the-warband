IRONWOOD AUDIO — LICENSE AND PROVENANCE

The music and the combat one-shots under assets/audio/sfx were produced by the
project from the recordings listed in docs/audio.md ("Slicing"). The spoken
lines under assets/audio/voice are cut from the two Freesound recordings below.

Both recordings are Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0):

CC BY 4.0 permits copying, modifying and redistributing the work, including
commercially, provided the creator is credited and the changes are indicated.
Credits and changes are recorded here and in docs/audio.md.

WOLF GROWL
  "wolf-growl.wav" by newagesoup
  https://freesound.org/people/newagesoup/sounds/338674/
  (c) newagesoup, licensed CC BY 4.0
  Source file: 338674__newagesoup__wolf-growl.mp3 (the creator's 48 kHz preview
  master, https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/338/338674_4067257-hq.mp3)
  Changes: trimmed, mono-mixed and normalised; three windows of the sustained
  growl were cut into assets/audio/voice/wolf/growl_01..03.ogg. The game
  additionally pitches each take down or up by up to 6% and shifts it to the
  body's position.

WOLF DEATH
  "Dog Begging.wav" by Coral_Island_Studios
  https://freesound.org/people/Coral_Island_Studios/sounds/277195/
  (c) Coral_Island_Studios, licensed CC BY 4.0
  Source file: 277195__coral-island-studios__dog-begging.mp3 (the creator's
  48 kHz preview master,
  https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/277/277195_5324256-hq.mp3)
  Changes: trimmed, mono-mixed and normalised; six separate vocalisations were
  cut out of the take into assets/audio/voice/wolf/death_01..06.ogg and are
  played pitched down 10-20% so they read as a dying wolf rather than a dog.

Both windows tables live in tools/slice_game_audio.py (VOICE_SLICED), which is
what turns the two masters into the shipped .ogg files; `--fetch` downloads the
masters from the URLs above, so the tree is reproducible without an account.

THIRD-PARTY SOUND SOURCES — THE ONE-SHOTS

The combat one-shots under assets/audio/sfx are slices of the nine Freesound
recordings listed in docs/audio.md ("Slicing"), which records every window and
every output file. Two of the nine are licensed CC BY, and the credits below are
required wherever this game is distributed, a commercial build included:

SWORD THROAT SLIT — "sword_throat_slitting_sound_10032026" by Artninja
  https://freesound.org/people/Artninja/sounds/873151/
  (c) Artninja, licensed Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  Changes: sixteen takes cut to the eight kept, trimmed, mono-mixed, peak
  normalised and pitched per take -> assets/audio/sfx/impact/slit_01..08.ogg

ARROW — "Arrow.wav" by EverHeat
  https://freesound.org/people/EverHeat/sounds/205563/
  (c) EverHeat, licensed Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

  assets/audio/sfx/bow/arrow_01.ogg

required and each is credited anyway:
