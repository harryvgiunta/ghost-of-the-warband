LOCAL PREPARATION

Outputs: ironwood_ranger.glb and ironwood_ranger.json (measured integration data).
Reproducible preparation: tools/prepare_character.py and its
tools/character_clearance.py helper (Blender 5.1+).
Source downloads and preparation/inspection .blend files are stored under the
ignored .runtime/character-source directory, not redistributed as giant packs.
The shipped GLB contains only the clothed male model, selected authored motions,
skin and required embedded textures (resized to a maximum of 1024 pixels).
All animation uses one Ranger skeleton. Rest-axis rotation retargeting preserves
source whole-body movement and the attacking arm's original elbow bend. The
Gres source has different limb proportions. A two-bone support-palm cleanup
holds the left palm on the source-derived hilt, correcting the difference
between source mitten and Ranger finger/palm proportions. Minimal support-
clavicle elevation supplies anatomical reach during the high load, without
stretching bones. Fingers use the Quaternius Sword_Idle right-hand authored
gripping pose, mirrored for the left hand, instead of an unmapped mitten.

Clothed-rig clearance cleanup affects only the two-handed support arm in
Sword_Idle and Sword_Overhead. The left elbow pole is moved outward while
keeping the wrist on the hilt; low recovery additionally uses at most 20 degrees
of support-clavicle depression and a smooth palm slide from 0.12 to 0.08 metres
along the existing handle. The pose search uses actual deformed Male_Ranger_Body
triangles, not an estimated torso capsule. A continuity-penalized path and
short symmetric smoothing avoid frame-by-frame pole switching. Anatomical bone
lengths, attacking right-arm/blade trajectory, torso/leg motion, source timing,
and one-handed clips are preserved. No runtime sword IK is required.

The exported GLB is reimported into Blender and inspected at 240 Hz: both
forearms' centerlines and +/-30 mm offsets along BOTH perpendicular axes must
clear the actual skinned shirt. Idle looping, guard-to-windup and recovery-to-
guard interpolations are also inspected. The support palm must remain on the
handle. Measured correction ranges and validation results are retained in
ironwood_ranger.json. No programmatically invented attack pose sequence is used.

Bow-specific authored draw/release clips were not included in the downloaded
free Standard libraries; bow interaction is a gameplay skeletal overlay rather
than a mislabeled asset clip.

Cover_Lean_Left, Cover_Lean_Right and Cover_Lean_Top are original local additive
cover poses authored reproducibly by tools/prepare_character.py, not renamed
source attacks. All begin at the identical retargeted Idle_Loop frame zero.
Smooth weight transfer keys pelvis counterbalance, three articulated spine
joints and modest neck/head counterbalance; arm-local transforms remain neutral
so attack/aim overlays retain their grip. Fixed-length two-bone leg solves keep
the original ankles and foot orientations planted. These local additions are
distributed under the same CC0 1.0 dedication linked above.

Exported asset axes are +Y up and +Z forward. Anatomical left leans toward +X
and right toward -X, each reaching 18 degrees chest tilt; top leans toward +Z
at 14 degrees. The pelvis countertranslates 0.02 metres, counterrotates by
3 degrees and lowers 0.012 metres for knee reach. The three spine joints reach
50/80/100 percent of chest tilt; neck/head reduce chest tilt by 2/5 degrees.
Each non-looping clip contains 16 full-hierarchy samples;
its GLB input times are uniformly retimed to 0.24 seconds after the normal
60 Hz bake (avoiding a rounded 14.4-frame endpoint). Existing combat values,
source timings, clearance corrections and sockets are unaffected by retiming.

Crouch clips use the same Quaternius rest-axis retargeting as other locomotion.
JSON records their actual duration, mean/range of pelvis lowering relative to
Idle_Loop frame zero, and ankle fore/aft excursion. Crouch_Fwd_Loop nominal
speed and full-cycle stride estimate use median backwards ankle speed during
ground contact within 25 mm of the lowest ankle, multiplied by cycle duration.
The in-place source has no root-distance track; this is an explicitly measured
contact-velocity estimate, not a claimed source root-motion distance.

IRONWOOD BRIAR WOLF — ASSET LICENSE AND PROVENANCE

The wolf asset is original procedural work authored for this repository. It
does not derive from the Quaternius/Gres Ranger sources above, and their CC0
licences do not cover it. No third-party model, texture, motion capture or
reference asset is included.

Authoring source: tools/prepare_wolf.py (deterministic Blender Python).
Authoring tool: Blender 5.1.2 (build 2026-05-19), scene unit metres, 60 fps.
Runtime engine: Godot 4.7.2.
Runtime outputs:
  assets/characters/ironwood_wolf.glb
  assets/characters/ironwood_wolf.json (measured integration metadata)
  assets/characters/ironwood_wolf.glb.import (embedded image handling 2)

The wolf GLB embeds one 1024 x 1024 sRGB albedo texture painted directly into
the UV atlas from rest-space anatomy. It contains a continuous body/neck/head,
separate lower jaw, ears, eyes, nose, teeth, claws and paw pads. The mesh has
15,036 triangles, 37 skeleton bones and nine material surfaces. Exported bounds
are approximately 0.537 x 1.176 x 2.218 metres with the paws at y=0; exported
forward is -Z and up is +Y, matching gameplay without a wrapper rotation.

Authored clips (60 Hz baked, in-place):
  Wolf_Idle_Loop  2.40 s, loop
  Wolf_Trot_Loop  0.60 s, loop, 1.15 m full-cycle stride, 0.45 stance duty
  Wolf_Attack     1.50 s, non-loop, authored impact at 0.65 s
  Wolf_Death      0.45 s, non-loop, final collapse pose held by the runtime

The runtime presentation is scripts/wolf_visual.gd; the wolf branch of
scripts/enemy.gd owns only AI/collision/damage and samples the attack from its
existing 0.65 s windup and 0.85 s recovery timers. No clip event deals damage.

The wolf is distributed under CC0 1.0 Universal, the same dedication linked at
the top of this file, to match the project's existing character assets.

Reproduce with:
  blender --background --python-exit-code 1 --python tools/prepare_wolf.py -- \
      .runtime/character-source/wolf assets/characters/ironwood_wolf.glb
  Godot_v4.7.2-stable_win64_console.exe --headless --editor --path . --import

A fresh Blender run reproduces the measured geometry, skeleton, clip timings
and coat pattern; byte-identical output is not promised because Smart UV
