LOCAL PREPARATION

Outputs: ironwood_ranger.glb and ironwood_ranger.json (measured integration data).
Reproducible preparation: tools/prepare_character.py and its
tools/character_clearance.py helper (Blender 5.1+).
Source downloads and preparation/inspection .blend files are stored under the
ignored .runtime/character-source directory, not redistributed as giant packs.
The shipped GLB contains only the clothed male model, selected authored motions,
skin and required embedded textures (resized to a maximum of 1024 pixels).
All animation uses one Ranger skeleton. Rest-axis rotation retargeting preserves
source whole-body movement and the attacking arm's original elbow bend. The
Gres source has different limb proportions. A two-bone support-palm cleanup
holds the left palm on the source-derived hilt, correcting the difference
between source mitten and Ranger finger/palm proportions. Minimal support-
clavicle elevation supplies anatomical reach during the high load, without
stretching bones. Fingers use the Quaternius Sword_Idle right-hand authored
gripping pose, mirrored for the left hand, instead of an unmapped mitten.

Clothed-rig clearance cleanup affects only the two-handed support arm in
Sword_Idle and Sword_Overhead. The left elbow pole is moved outward while
keeping the wrist on the hilt; low recovery additionally uses at most 20 degrees
of support-clavicle depression and a smooth palm slide from 0.12 to 0.08 metres
along the existing handle. The pose search uses actual deformed Male_Ranger_Body
triangles, not an estimated torso capsule. A continuity-penalized path and
short symmetric smoothing avoid frame-by-frame pole switching. Anatomical bone
lengths, attacking right-arm/blade trajectory, torso/leg motion, source timing,
and one-handed clips are preserved. No runtime sword IK is required.

The exported GLB is reimported into Blender and inspected at 240 Hz: both
forearms' centerlines and +/-30 mm offsets along BOTH perpendicular axes must
clear the actual skinned shirt. Idle looping, guard-to-windup and recovery-to-
guard interpolations are also inspected. The support palm must remain on the
handle. Measured correction ranges and validation results are retained in
ironwood_ranger.json. No programmatically invented attack pose sequence is used.

Bow-specific authored draw/release clips were not included in the downloaded
free Standard libraries; bow interaction is a gameplay skeletal overlay rather
than a mislabeled asset clip.

Cover_Lean_Left, Cover_Lean_Right and Cover_Lean_Top are original local additive
cover poses authored reproducibly by tools/prepare_character.py, not renamed
source attacks. All begin at the identical retargeted Idle_Loop frame zero.
Smooth weight transfer keys pelvis counterbalance, three articulated spine
joints and modest neck/head counterbalance; arm-local transforms remain neutral
so attack/aim overlays retain their grip. Fixed-length two-bone leg solves keep
the original ankles and foot orientations planted. These local additions are
part of the shipped asset and are licensed with it under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0
(LICENSE.md, section 3).

Exported asset axes are +Y up and +Z forward. Anatomical left leans toward +X
and right toward -X, each reaching 18 degrees chest tilt; top leans toward +Z
at 14 degrees. The pelvis countertranslates 0.02 metres, counterrotates by
3 degrees and lowers 0.012 metres for knee reach. The three spine joints reach
50/80/100 percent of chest tilt; neck/head reduce chest tilt by 2/5 degrees.
Each non-looping clip contains 16 full-hierarchy samples;
its GLB input times are uniformly retimed to 0.24 seconds after the normal
60 Hz bake (avoiding a rounded 14.4-frame endpoint). Existing combat values,
source timings, clearance corrections and sockets are unaffected by retiming.

Crouch clips use the same Quaternius rest-axis retargeting as other locomotion.
JSON records their actual duration, mean/range of pelvis lowering relative to
Idle_Loop frame zero, and ankle fore/aft excursion. Crouch_Fwd_Loop nominal
speed and full-cycle stride estimate use median backwards ankle speed during
ground contact within 25 mm of the lowest ankle, multiplied by cycle duration.
The in-place source has no root-distance track; this is an explicitly measured
contact-velocity estimate, not a claimed source root-motion distance.

IRONWOOD NAKED BODY — SOURCE AND PROVENANCE

The body the warband wears when it wears nothing is a real anatomical sculpt,
not a modelled tube. It is the male base body from the same CC0 pack that
supplies the Ranger's head:

  Quaternius — Universal Base Characters [Standard]
  https://quaternius.com/packs/universalbasecharacters.html
  https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-base-characters
  Original archive: Universal Base Characters[Standard].zip
  Original selected model:
    Base Characters/Godot - UE/Superhero_Male_FullBody.gltf
    Superhero_Male_FullBody.bin and the T_Superhero_Male_* base-colour,
    normal and roughness maps
  Licence: License_Standard.txt, CC0 1.0 Universal

The source mesh carries the same 65-bone rest skeleton as the Ranger, with bone
names, rest transforms, skin weights and UVs. It is used as the naked set's
skin: split by dominant bone into the four slot pieces
(Armor_Naked_Chest/_Hands/_Legs/_Feet), bound to the Ranger armature, and
exported with the source's own 2048 base-colour, normal and roughness maps
through its own UV layout. The ring, wrist and ankle seams of the four pieces
share the body's own smoothed normals, so they read as one continuous body.
The source's head, eyes and brows are not used here: the Ranger head stays on
screen outside every armour slot, so the naked body ends in a neck.

Original modifications, all authored reproducibly in Blender:
  - the source head is removed at z = 1.565 m and the neck is tapered to 0.87
    of its radius so it enters the Ranger head's opening (measured against the
    head's own lower shell; the cut ring clears it by ~9-18 mm);
  - the ribcage is slimmed by up to 12% in depth and 3.5% in width with a
    smooth height-weighted falloff centred at z = 1.30 m, which removes the
    superhero barrel chest and restores human chest/waist proportion without
    touching the arms, neck or waist;
  - the shirt and shorts are cut from the body's own surface (the same faces),
    relaxed, pushed outside the skin by 10-24 mm, given outward-only drape
    folds, finished with extruded hem/cuff/waist bands and solidified to 3.5 mm
    (shirt) and 4.0 mm (shorts);
  - the cloth maps are rasterised per texel at bind-space positions: a linen
    plain weave for the shirt (4.2 mm pitch) with a hem stitch, button placket
    and ribbed crew collar, and a charcoal twill for the shorts (5.4 mm pitch)
    with waistband, leg hems, side seam and fly, plus slubs, dye drift and
    grime that collects at the hems;
  - the skin keeps the source's painted pores, knuckle creases and nails; no
    colour was invented for it.

The set is 17,082 triangles, four pieces, three materials (Naked_Skin,
Naked_Shirt, Naked_Shorts), nine embedded maps and the Ranger's 65 bones. The
JSON records the measured neck taper, torso slim, garment extents and gaps.

Reproduce with (after unpacking the pack into .runtime/character-source):
  python tools/build_naked_armor.py
  Godot_v4.7.2-stable_win64_console.exe --headless --editor --path . --import

IRONWOOD ARMOR — ASSET LICENSE AND PROVENANCE

The swappable armor pieces are original procedural work authored for this
repository. The mail, plate and Emberweave cloth sets, the leather gloves and
the dark hose underlayer do not derive from any additional third-party source;
the CC0 Quaternius Ranger outfit above supplies the leather hood, jerkin, belt,
pauldron, bracers, trousers and boots that are grouped by slot at runtime.

Authoring source: tools/character_armor.py (deterministic Blender Python),
called by tools/prepare_character.py after the Ranger animations are baked.
Authoring tool: Blender 5.1.2 (build 2026-05-19), scene unit metres, 60 fps.
Runtime outputs (inside the Ranger GLB/JSON):
  Armor_<Set>_<Slot> for set in Mail, Plate, Cloth and slot in Head, Chest,
  Hands, Legs, Feet; Armor_Leather_Hands; Ranger_Base_Calf
  armor.generated metadata in ironwood_ranger.json
  embedded 512 x 512 sRGB albedo and normal textures generated in Blender

The pieces are skinned to the Ranger's 65-bone rest skeleton with explicit
weights, authored in armature space (+X character left, -Y forward, +Z up) and
exported in the same GLB as the animated body. The base calf is always visible
so short sabatons can be mixed with the short leather trousers without an empty
shin. The leather outfit is the licensed Quaternius mesh above, split by node
name; no pixels or vertices from a restricted source are used.

Reproduce with:
  blender --background --python-exit-code 1 --python tools/prepare_character.py -- \
      .runtime/character-source assets/characters/ironwood_ranger.glb
  Godot_v4.7.2-stable_win64_console.exe --headless --editor --path . --import

The armor is distributed under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (LICENSE.md, section 3): the
Quaternius leather underneath stays CC0, the generated mail, plate, cloth and
hose do not.

IRONWOOD WARBAND LOOKS — SOURCE AND PROVENANCE

The warband's eight faces, nine haircuts, four beards and its tintable skin,
shirt and shorts surfaces are derived, reproducibly, from assets this repository
already ships. Nothing is downloaded for them and nothing is modelled by hand:

    python tools/prepare_warband_looks.py

Inputs
  assets/characters/ironwood_ranger.glb
    head (Sphere.005_Retopology.004), brows (Face) and eyes (Face.001) meshes,
    their skin weights and joint indices, the T_Superhero_Male_Normal and
    T_Eye_* maps, and the T_Eye_Brown iris. The Ranger's own provenance above
    applies: Quaternius Universal Base Characters, CC0 1.0 Universal.
  assets/characters/armor/ironwood_naked_T_Superhero_Male_Dark.png
  assets/characters/armor/ironwood_naked_Naked_Shirt_albedo.png
  assets/characters/armor/ironwood_naked_Naked_Shorts_albedo.png
    the naked set's skin, linen and twill atlases, from the same CC0 pack and
    the same procedurally-rasterised cloth as the naked set above.

Outputs and what was done to the inputs
  assets/characters/warband/ironwood_faces.glb
    eight variants, each an analytic warp of the union of the three head
    meshes: jaw width, chin length and width, cheekbone spread, nose projection
    and width, brow ridge, eye spacing, mouth width and lip volume. The warp is
    windowed to the front of the face so the braincase, and therefore every
    helmet and hood in the armour sets, keeps its authored dimensions. UVs,
    joint indices and skin weights are the source's; the meshes bind to the live
    Ranger skeleton at runtime by repointing their skin. No other geometry,
    material or clip of the Ranger asset is touched.
  assets/characters/warband/ironwood_hair.glb
    original geometry authored by the tool: a short cut is a thin shell of the skull's own triangles,
    wrapped flush on the head; the afro is the one lofted hair mass, from a crown
    disc down to its own measured hairline and settled flush onto the skin, and a beard is a sheet over the jaw cut out by its own alpha
    design - cheek line, arched moustache, open mouth, jawline bottom edge. All
    of it is bound rigidly to the Ranger's `Head` joint.
  assets/characters/warband/warband_skin_basecolor.png (2048)
  assets/characters/warband/warband_skin_head_basecolor.png (1024)
  assets/characters/warband/warband_shirt_basecolor.png
  assets/characters/warband/warband_shorts_basecolor.png
    "veiled" derivatives of the atlases above: each was converted to linear
    light, divided by the mean of its brightest fifth (the flat lit surface),
    partly desaturated so a pale character's blush does not survive every skin
    tone, and re-encoded as sRGB. The artist's pores, knit, twill, grime and
    folds survive; the hue does not, which is what lets one map serve eight skin
    tones and eight dyed shirts through a single `albedo_color`.
  assets/characters/warband/warband_hair_basecolor.png
  assets/characters/warband/warband_beard_<style>.png
    original maps generated analytically by the tool: a soft, painterly hair
    albedo (no baked strands - at gameplay distance fine strands read as
    scratches over the skull), and one RGBA mask per beard carrying its own
    silhouette and mouth cut.
  assets/characters/warband/warband_eye_<n>_basecolor.png
    five recolours of the Ranger's authored brown iris; sclera, pupil and the
    eye's own normal map are the source's.
  assets/characters/warband/ironwood_warband_looks.json
    the variant tables, the tone palettes (entry 0 of each cloth/skin palette is
    the measured base colour of the shipped texture), the measured head
    landmarks and the file budgets. `WarbandLooks` reads it at runtime, so the
    tool output is the single source of truth for what a mercenary can wear.
