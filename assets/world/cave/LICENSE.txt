IRONWOOD CAVE KIT (THE WEEPING HOLLOW) - LICENSE AND PROVENANCE

Everything under this directory (assets/world/cave/) is built by one command:

    python tools/build_cave_kit.py

which downloads the pinned sources below into the ignored .runtime/cave-source/,
checks each against its SHA-256 (tools/cave_kit_sources.py), and runs Blender 5.1
in background mode on tools/prepare_cave_kit.py. cave_kit.json records every
source URL, digest and author, the Blender version, and the digest of every
output.

WHAT WAS MADE FROM THEM

  textures/cave_<set>_{albedo,normal,orh}.png  (limestone = Rock Face 03,
      strata = Cliff Side, basalt = Dark Rock 02, silt = Rocks Ground 08):
      averaged to 1024, broad blotching flattened, desaturated and normalised to
      one mean albedo; normals renormalised and rescaled; ORH packs ambient
      occlusion, roughness and percentile-normalised height.
  cave_formations.glb: thirteen original dripstone and breakdown pieces sculpted
      from analytic fields in Blender; base colour, normals, roughness and
      occlusion baked in Cycles from the high-detail sculpt, with Rock 08
      box-projected into the source surface.
  cave_flora.glb: three original fungus pieces (a giant cap, a cap cluster, a
      bracket shelf), baked the same way from procedural Blender surfaces only.
  cave_fauna.glb: original bat, rat, cave spider and moth meshes, vertex
      coloured; no third-party source.
  textures/cave_web.png: an original web built as curves and rendered in Cycles.
